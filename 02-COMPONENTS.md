4|## BASE SYSTEM
5|| Component | Recommendation | Why | Alternatives Considered |
6||---|---|---|---|
7|| Base distro | Debian (testing) | Smallest base, apt ecosystem, snap/flatpak support, no Canonical | Ubuntu (heavier, Canonical telemetry), Alpine (no snap/flatpak) |
8|| Kernel | Linux + KSPP hardening | Vanilla kernel with security-focused config, AppArmor MAC | grsecurity (paid), Linux-libre (hardware issues) |
9|| Init system | systemd | Standard, well-supported, namespaces/cgroups for service isolation | OpenRC (less features), runit (less ecosystem) |
10|| Bootloader | systemd-boot | Simple, UEFI-native | GRUB (more complex, more attack surface) |
11|| Desktop | GNOME 46+ | Most polished, Wayland-native, extensions for customization | KDE (heavier customization), COSMIC (immature) |

13|## NETWORK LAYER
14|| Component | Recommendation | Why | Alternatives Considered |
15||---|---|---|---|
16|| Mesh network | Yggdrasil | Encrypted IPv6 mesh, NAT traversal, stable addresses from keys, mature | cjdns (less maintained), tinc (manual config), ZeroTier (centralized) |
17|| DNS (mesh) | Custom DHT overlay on Yggdrasil | Federation of name→address records, no blockchain needed | Handshake (blockchain overhead), mDNS (local only) |
18|| DNS (regular) | Unbound | DNS-over-HTTPS, DNSSEC validation, caching resolver | systemd-resolved (less features), Pi-hole (additional service) |
19|| Firewall | nftables + AppArmor | Kernel-native, well-supported, mesh traffic isolated | UFW (higher level, less control), iptables (deprecated) |

21|## IDENTITY & ENCRYPTION
22|| Component | Recommendation | Why | Alternatives Considered |
23||---|---|---|---|
24|| Identity keys | Ed25519 (signing) + X25519 (encryption) | Fast, small, NaCl-standard, widely supported | RSA (larger keys), secp256k1 (Bitcoin-associated) |
25|| DID format | Custom: did:mesh:{public-key-hash} | Simple, self-sovereign, no blockchain needed | did:key (W3C standard), did:web (requires DNS) |
26|| Disk encryption | LUKS2 (AES-256-XTS) | Standard Linux FDE, TPM-assignable | VeraCrypt (extra software), BitLocker (Windows) |
27|| File encryption | age encryption | Modern, simple, banner than GPG | GPG (complex UX), Minilock (less maintained) |
28|| Email encryption | OpenPGP (autogenerated at setup) | Standard, interop with external email | Age (not email-native), Signal Protocol (not for email) |
29|| Network encryption | Yggdrasil (built-in) + TLS 1.3 | Ygg encrypts mesh traffic, TLS for internet traffic | WireGuard (point-to-point, not mesh) |

31|## SERVICES
32|| Component | Recommendation | Why | Alternatives Considered |
33||---|---|---|---|
34|| Email/Messaging server | Maddy | Single binary, IMAP+SMTP, low resource | Postfix+Dovecot (complex), Haraka (Node.js), Courier (heavy) |
35|| Web hosting | Caddy | Auto-HTTPS, simple config, HTTP/2, small binary | Nginx (more config), Apache (heavy) |
36|| Social feed protocol | Custom (Nostr-like signed events) | Simple, lightweight, built for this use case | ActivityPub (complex, separate servers per service), AT Protocol (complex) |
37|| Social feed datastore | SQLite + SSB-style logs | Local-first, append-only log, efficient queries | PostgreSQL (overkill), LevelDB (no SQL) |
38|| Content addressing | IPFS (Kubo) or custom CIDs | Content-addressed storage, deduplication, CDN-ready | BitTorrent (less flexible), custom (more work) |
39|| CDN agent | Custom Rust/Go daemon | CDN pinning, bandwidth management, reporting | IPFS Cluster (close but not exactly what we need) |
40|| Package management | apt + Flatpak + snap | Full Linux app compat, Flatpak for sandboxing | Nix (different paradigm), AppImage (no sandboxing) |

42|## DESKTOP & UX
43|| Component | Recommendation | Why | Alternatives Considered |
44||---|---|---|---|
45|| App framework | Progressive Web Apps (PWAs) | Your HTML/CSS/JS philosophy, offline capable, single codebase | GTK (native but complex), Qt (heavy), Electron (bloated) |
46|| Settings/Setup | Custom GTK4 app | Native feel for OOBE wizard | Web-based (less polished), Calamares (generic) |
47|| Notification daemon | GNOME built-in | Standard, works | Custom (reinventing wheel) |
48|| App store | GNOME Software + custom repo | Flatpak/snap/deb in one UI | Discover (KDE), Apper (less maintained) |

51|| Component | Choice | Why |
52||---|---|---|
53|| OS image builder | Debos + live-build | Debian-standard, reproducible builds |
54|| Service development | Rust | Memory-safe, fast, low resource — perfect for network services |
55|| PWA development | Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS | Aligned with your philosophy |
56|| Configuration management | Custom declarative YAML/TOML | OS settings, mesh config, service parameters |
