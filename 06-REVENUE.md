1|# Sovrn — Revenue Model
2|
3|## REVENUE STREAMS
4|
5|### 1. CDN Storage & Bandwidth (PRIMARY)
6|**How it works:**
7|- Anyone can run a CDN node (decentralized)
8|- Users pay CDN operators for storage + bandwidth
9|- The OS project takes a 5-10% facilitation fee from transactions
10|- Content is encrypted — CDN operators can't read it, just store and serve it
11|- Pricing is market-driven: CDN operators compete on price, reliability, bandwidth
12|
13|**Tiers (offered by CDN operators, not enforced by Sovrn):**
14|- Free tier: ~1 GB storage, limited bandwidth (enough for text + some images)
15|- Basic: ₹100-200/month for 10-50 GB storage
16|- Pro: ₹500-1000/month for 100-500 GB + higher bandwidth
17|- Enterprise: Custom pricing for heavy content creators
18|
19|**Revenue for the project:** 5-10% commission on all CDN transactions processed through Sovrn's built-in payment system.
20|
21|### 2. Premium Domain Auctions (SECONDARY)
22|- Standard .sovrn domains: free with rate limits (5/month 1, 3/month 2, 2/month after)
23|- Premium/short domains (1-3 characters, dictionary words): auctioned periodically
24|- Creates revenue without compromising the free tier for normal users
25|
26|### 3. Hardware (FUTURE)
27|- Sell pre-configured "mesh nodes" — Raspberry Pi or mini PC with Sovrn pre-installed
28|- Target: users who want an always-on node without technical setup
29|- Revenue: hardware margin
30|- Also: branded USB drives with OS installer + recovery tools
31|
32|### 4. Support & Consulting (FUTURE)
33|- Paid support for businesses/organizations deploying mesh networks
34|- Custom deployment, consulting, managed CDN services
35|
36|## WHAT'S FREE
37|- The OS itself: free download, free to use
38|- Basic mesh services: DNS, messaging, social feed, homepage hosting
39|- Up to 5 .sovrn domains: free
40|- All core functionality: zero cost forever
41|
42|## WHAT'S PAID
43|- CDN storage (optional, for content creators and people who want always-on availability)
44|- Premium domain names (optional, vanity)
45|- Hardware (optional, convenience)
46|- Support (optional, for businesses)
47|
48|## PAYMENT METHODS
49|- Phase 1: UPI, credit/debit card (India-focused, accessible)
50|- Phase 2: Add cryptocurrency (Monero for maximum privacy, Bitcoin for accessibility)
51|- In-app: built-in payment settings, choose CDN provider, pay, done
52|
53|## ECONOMICS PROJECTION (ROUGH)
54|Assuming 10,000 active users in year 1:
55|- 20% use CDN (2,000 users)
56|- Average CDN spend: ₹200/month
57|- Monthly revenue: 2,000 × ₹200 × 10% = ₹4,000/month (commission)
58|- This is minimal — the real revenue comes at scale
59|
60|Assuming 100,000 users:
61|- 20% use CDN (20,000 users)
62|- Average CDN spend: ₹300/month
63|- Monthly revenue: 20,000 × ₹300 × 10% = ₹6,00,000/month
64|
65|Assuming 1,000,000 users:
66|- 20% use CDN (200,000 users)
67|- Average CDN spend: ₹300/month
68|- Monthly revenue: 200,000 × ₹300 × 10% = ₹60,00,000/month
69|
70|**Key insight:** Revenue scales with user count and content creation. The OS is free, CDN is the monetization.
